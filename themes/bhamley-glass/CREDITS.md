# Credits

The three pictures in `wallpapers/` (`morning.jpg`, `evening.jpg` and `night.jpg`) are drawn in code by
`tools/make_wallpapers.py` in the Nokia_andriod_tv repo (numpy, scipy and Pillow): gradients, soft light, silk ribbons and
glass orbs, with a fixed random seed. No photos and no third-party art are used. They are released under CC0: use them,
change them, ship them.
